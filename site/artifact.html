How our picks work
  • Every hour we pull the latest stats, injury reports, weather, news and betting lines.
  • Our model predicts every game and compares it to what the sportsbooks are offering.
  • Final picks lock 24 hours before kickoff and count toward our record. Before that you'll see an early pick that can still change.
  • Value rates the price: Strong, Good or Some. "No bet" means the line looks fair.
  • Hot trends are streaks, like a player going over a line in 4 of his last 5 games or a team covering 3 straight. Streaks can end any week, so we also tell you whether our model agrees.
  • Best player props are the players whose matchup this week pushes them furthest from their usual numbers. Always check the line at your sportsbook before betting.

Picks are predictions for entertainment and information, not guarantees. Never bet more than you can afford to lose. If gambling stops being fun, call 1-800-GAMBLER.
